Meet your trainer

Trainer Anne Baldwin

Dedicated to evidence-based, fear-free training techniques

I use positive reinforcement techniques because they are proven to be the most effective and ethical. Not just in books and statistics, but in real life with real dogs and people. I am continually expanding my knowledge and skills, so you can trust you have expert in your corner.

Meeting you where you're at with empathy & compassion

I'm not here to judge or criticize. Like any dog owner, I've made mis-steps and I've learned from every one of them. I'm here to listen with kindness and compassion, and guide you with expertise and optimism every step of the way. It's also important to me that any training plan feels achievable and fit to your lifestyle.

Customizing a training plan to match you and your dog

I provide clear, easy to follow dog training guidance customized to your preferred learning style using videos, writing, and progress tracking. I combine training sessions where I coach the human with training sessions one-on-one with your dog so that your dog's skills get a jump start that you can run with.

Getting Started

How We Work Together

  1. Free 30 minute consultation

    Every partnership starts with a free, 30 minute consultation. Tell me about your dog and your goals and I'll tell you what I would recommend.

  2. Optional customized training plan — $100

    If you want to move forward, I can put together a customized written training, management, and enrichment plan for $100 after this consultation. This $100 is credited to a package if you move forward with me, but you have no committment to move forward at this point.

  3. Purchase a package of training credits

    When you purchase a package, the credits may be interchangeably used for any private services — anything except workshops, day school, or boarding. The training plan will recommend which combination of session types would be most effective for your dog and your goals, but we can adjust this to fit your needs and preferences.

  4. As needed support after your package

    After the initial package, you can book any kind of private training session for the package price, paying as you go. After two months of no Barking Bear services (boarding, hiking, and day school count!), your package pricing expires. But you are always welcome to book a one-off session at our standard price or start a new training plan and package.

Private Dog Training

Private Dog Training

In your home, virtually, or at the ranch

Sessions to diagnose behavior, coach the humans, and train your dog together.

In these sessions, Anne works with you, your family and your dog to:

  • Come to understand the "why" behind both your dog's behavior
  • Learn and practice behavior modification exercises with your dog
  • Discuss enrichment and management techniques to mitigate undesired behaviors
  • Address behaviors like jumping, leash pulling, impolite dog-dog interactions, nuisance barking, resource guarding, house soiling, and more

Sessions can take place in one of three locations depending on your goals:

  • In your home, to target context specific behaviors and learn skills in the most familiar low distraction environment.
  • At Barking Bear Ranch, especially conducive to working on trail skills, recall, and dog-dog reactivity (we can use a neutral "decoy dog" when needed).
  • Virtually, for convenience and a discount, with more frequent check-ins and lots of video exchange.

Doggie Private Tutoring

Foundational skills, practiced at home

Private sessions for your dog to learn and practice skills in your home.

Often called "day training" by other trainers, these hour long sessions are for your dog — your attendance is optional. Training takes place in your home, which is best when your dog is in the early stages of learning a new skill, or when we're working on context specific behaviors that only happen in your home.

  • Get extra practice learning new skills, manners, or behaviors - without extra time and effort from you
  • Guide your dog through the initial learning process so you just have to maintain the new behaviors, not teach them from the ground up
  • Great for basic obedience and tricks, confidence building, early recall and leash skills, and relaxation protocols
  • A great option to add on to Private Dog Training to accelerate your dog's learning progress

Training for Your Dog

Doggie Field Trips

Private one hour excursions to get your dog socialization and structured practice in public settings.

For puppies, field trips help them grow up confidently in this exciting, overwhelming, and sometimes scary world. The best time for puppy socialization is 3-16 weeks, but the second best time is NOW! Don't wait until your pup is fully vaccinated to start!

  • Outings crafted around your goals and matched to your puppy's personality
  • Gentle exposure and desensitization to common "scary" sights and sounds
  • Practice polite greetings of humans and dogs in real world settings
  • A great way to get some of your puppy's crazy energy out while you get a break
  • We recommend as many sessions as possible while your dog is under 16 weeks!

For adult dogs, field trips are an advanced level training session where we use novel and public environments to strengthen their learned skills around distractions. This is known as "proofing" your dog's skills: practicing them in lots of variations and contexts to solidify them.

  • Work on patio restaurant and brewery manners
  • Work on leash manners in a busy park or at a trailhead
  • Practice polite greetings of humans and dogs in real world settings

Doggie Private School

Doggie Private School

Half day of one-on-one training at The Ranch

One on one training for your dog to learn foundational skills at The Ranch.

Private School is one-on-one training at The Barking Bear Ranch for dogs who need extra repetitions of skills outside of the home. Sessions are 3 hours, with lots of break time built in. Examples of when Private School might be a better fit versus in-home tutoring:

  • Your dog needs extra repetitions of skills, but learns best with multiple short training sessions rather than an hour long tutoring session
  • Your training focus is on outdoor skills for hiking or prey drive, best to be practiced in a natural environment
  • Your dog is learning early dog-dog social skills and needs practicing with proper one-on-one introductions to carefully chosen dogs
  • Before joining the Doggie Day School group, your dog needs to learn some basic skills (place, wait, take a break, recall, leave it, engage/disengage and more)
  • Trial days in preparation for boarding - if your dog is sensitive to change and needs some supported time with us in our home to feel safe when it's time for boarding

Training for Your Dog

Doggie Day School

Training and enrichment with a small group of compatible dogs at The Ranch.

Traditional daycare is too hectic and too confined of an environment for many dogs - it's like sending your dog to a rave to learn how to socialize! Doggie Day School is intended to be an alternative to traditional daycare focused on learning healthy social skills and meeting your dog's needs with training and enrichment.

  • It is a consistent group of dogs on a weekly basis and play groups are kept to pairs or trios and monitored by a professional dog trainer to ensure that all play is polite, fun, and safe
  • Dog introductions are done gradually and thoughtfully with carefully selected partners and a 1:1 hander to dog ratio
  • Between play sessions, dogs get enrichment in a natural environment — sniffing, chewing, licking, shredding, digging, training, play, and rest to learn to regulate their excitement
  • Your dog gets extra "distraction-proofing" of skills such as recall, place, wait, leave it, engage/disengage, leash manners, arousal management, self regulation etc.
  • Week to week we choose a training topic designed to build confidence and meet your dog's mental and physical needs
  • Every school day you'll get a report card on your dog's progress with photos, instructions, and videos

New students start with Doggie Private School to build some basic skills and do some one on one dog-dog intros before joining a group setting.

Day School is currently fully booked by existing clients. Sign up for Private School to get on the wait list for when we re-open to new students.

Doggie Boarding School

Training during your dog's boarding stay

Board & train your dog to make the most of your vacation.

  • Immerse your dog in an environment full of opportunities to learn!
  • Open to all ages but an especially great option for puppies! Kick off a puppy fresh start with a solid foundation of skills and confidence!
  • Give your dog time to master skills like basic obedience, recall, crate training, mat work, loose leash walking, tricks, polite greetings, patio and brewery manners, and hiking skills at a comfortable, progressive pace
  • Minimum period of 10 days recommended, with a transfer of skills session to catch you up on what they learned and how to maintain their good habits
