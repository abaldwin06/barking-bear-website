In-Home Dog Boarding

A mountain getaway with all the comforts of home

Barking Bear Dog Boarding takes place in Anne & Dan's family home in Kittredge, CO, 30-45 min from Denver, which has:

  • A fully fenced acre for playtime & potty breaks
  • Ten acres of meadow & forest for hikes and zoomies.
  • Two dog-proofed bedrooms dedicated to canine guests.
  • A "rumble room" for play time with couches for naps.
  • Pet gated sections of the house to give dogs their own space until they are fully comfortable with one other.

Professional, insured pet sitters who treat your dog like family

The Barking Bear family is made up of:

  • Two dog behavior savvy, licensed, insured, and professional pet sitters, Dan & Anne, who both care for your pet and work from home.
  • Our three rescue pups, Ullie, Riley, and Ripley, and a rotating adoptable foster dog.
  • Our cat Strudel, who is kept separate from all dogs in the house.
  • A wonderful community of long-time customers, so your pup will see familiar friendly faces every stay.

Kenneled, VIP, and Special Needs boarding for all temperaments

  • All new clients start with a required trial overnight and stay at the VIP rate until we determine they are a better fit for another boarding type
  • Dogs stay as VIP guests as long as they are under 1 year, intact, kennel-free, or need to be kept fully separate from other dogs
  • Dogs with separation anxiety or severe fear, anxiety, or stress are accommodated as special needs guests
  • Vaccinated and licensed dogs of any breed, age, or size are welcome
  • Dog social, dog selective, and dog tolerant guests are welcome, if tolerant of dogs under the same roof
  • Guests who need meds, enrichment routines, or elaborate food preparation are welcomed
  • Pet parents with their own "separation anxiety", don't worry all pet parents get frequent photo and video updates with any boarding type!

We recommend dropping off by 12pm on your dog's first day so that your dog has time to adjust before bedtime — this also makes the most of our per day pricing model. * Boarding type is our evaluation of your dog's care needs when they stay with us. This may be different from what they need at home in your environment, and this may change over time as your dog gets more comfortable with us. This boarding level is adjusted on a stay-by-stay basis and we are happy to discuss our rationale and offer training guidance to address any special needs.

** Per Day pricing — We don't charge our boarding rate per 24 hours, or per night, we charge per calendar day. This means that you pay the full boarding rate for the day you drop off and the day you pick up, and all days in between. We have this pricing model to account for the fact that most boarding stays start early in the morning and end in the evening, but also because the first day and last days are always a little extra effort with our detailed cleaning protocols, slow dog-dog introductions, and the extra exercise, enrichment, and attention to help settle dogs' natural nervous energy during transitions. Also, we have a strict 3 dog capacity at our facility, so if your dog is at our facility on one day, we can't have another paying client fill their space that day. We do have an early last-day pickup policy where if you pick up your dog between 8 and 9am on their final day, there is no charge for the pickup day because we are able to clean and be ready for new clients by the time we'd like them to be dropped off.

*** The Ranch Regular discount is given on a case by case basis to dogs we know especially well who have been with us for a while. This is based on how your dog fits in with our pups, how much time they have spent boarding with us, how frequently they board with us, how many other services they use with us (training, hikes, playgroups), and discounts their boarding rate by 10%. This discount is available regardless of what boarding type you have, so even a Special Needs boarder may be a Ranch Regular.

**** We consider all our clients friends and family, but the Friends and Family rate is about 40% off our standard rates and is for dog families who were friends first and became clients second. We may situationally offer this rate to folks who are unable to pay their full rate due to financial circumstances.

Unfortunately, no sibling discount is currently offered. Due to our strict capacity of 3 boarding dogs at once, we do not currently have a specific discount for sibling pairs. We may re-introduce this in the future — thank you for your understanding!
